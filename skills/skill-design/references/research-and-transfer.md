# Research and transfer

Use for a new method, material extension or recurring failure. This reference
owns transfer into skill behavior; the available
[evidence-research](../../evidence-research/SKILL.md) method owns general search,
source checking, synthesis and stopping. Use it when substantive research is
unresolved, with one evidence record. Reuse sufficient verified prior evidence
for a narrow change; do not require the full research method for ordinary edits.

## Connect evidence to the protected decision

Map applicable decision families beyond the complaint list before claiming a
material workflow is covered. For an operational workflow this may include
entry, prerequisites, action, pending work, success/partial failure, recovery,
departure and return. Include relevant roles, environments or scale only where
they change the decision. A fixed checklist of product features is not the goal.

For each material transfer connect: decision and current gap; examined source
location and conditions; local procedure; legitimate neighboring case; observable
check; and adopt/adapt/retain/reject/defer decision. Distinguish sufficient
coverage, absent guidance, a generic rule with a weak check, inapplicability and
unexamined material. A package URL or more examples of one defect cannot fill
unrelated families. Consider a consequential combination outside reported
examples, without claiming that specifying it is executing it.

Separate outcome criteria from platform standards, implementation choices,
author preferences and execution policy. A successful external method does not
authorize importing its delegation, model routes, installations or paid runs.
Model-specific observations belong in evaluation evidence, not universal rules.

## Repair the responsible layer

Determine whether knowledge was absent, a branch was not loaded, an existing rule
was ignored or acceptance could pass with the defect intact. Use the available
trace; without it the cause remains a hypothesis. Improve routing when the branch
was missed and the oracle when the check was weak. Another paraphrase does not
establish that an execution failure is fixed.

Keep general decisions in the skill, concrete incidents in evaluations/history
and mechanical invariants in existing owner tools. Use ordinary authoring for
packaging. Prefer conditional `references/` with explicit reading conditions;
preserve a justified existing consumer path. Moving all mandatory reading out of
the entrypoint does not prove lower context use. Apply the parent skill's
evaluation method before claiming improvement, savings or portability.
