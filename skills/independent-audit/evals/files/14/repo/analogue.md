# Synthetic product reference: BatchDesk

This fictional hosted team product uses row-numbered validation messages, preview,
explicit confirmation, a completion summary, and a post-commit undo action. Teams
can also share imports, schedule recurring jobs, and synchronize their activity.
Its hosted SDK requires an account and sends imported data to its service.

These facts describe BatchDesk's design and deployment model within this exercise.
They do not establish the local project's requirements, license rights, measured
usability, integration compatibility, or permission to adopt the SDK.
