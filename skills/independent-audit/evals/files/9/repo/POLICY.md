# Product and development contract

The product prints its built-in greeting and must work from a clean checkout
without personal services, credentials, editor settings, or machine bindings.
The same development checks must remain usable when a contributor keeps optional
editor display preferences locally. The product does not use those preferences.

The published product contains `product.py`. Personal editor preferences stay
local and must not enter the distribution selection. The owner has not required
an otherwise empty workstation or the removal of unrelated local settings.
