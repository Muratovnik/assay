# Comparison and synthesis

Use for selecting options, explaining causes or transferring a discovered pattern.

## Compare the decision, not the publicity

Translate preferences into relevant criteria and separate hard constraints from
tradeoffs. Include the current solution when viable. Evaluate options on the
same scope and conditions; mark unavailable evidence instead of inventing a
score. An undocumented capability is unknown until examined, not automatically
absent. A maintained alternative is not itself a reason to replace working code.

For a material adoption consider integration, maintenance, licensing, security,
operating constraints and replacement/migration costs where they affect fit.
Different dependencies or more popular products need an in-scope benefit.
Learning from a product does not authorize adopting its code, service or features.
An approved product policy outranks an appealing external convention.

## Explain disagreement and causation

Separate disagreement about facts, definitions, methods, interpretation and time.
Check configurations, populations and benchmarks before treating different
measurements as incompatible. Seek evidence capable of changing the conclusion,
without requiring an opponent for an uncontested fact or manufacturing balance.

For a causal claim consider plausible alternatives and what observation would
distinguish them. Chronology, correlation or an actor's explanation may be useful
but insufficient. Do not infer a universal model limitation from a failure in one
configuration. Successful examples alone can hide failed or excluded cases.

Synthesize around questions and decisions rather than consecutive source
summaries. Each load-bearing recommendation should show how the evidence and
constraints imply it. State assumptions and how the recommendation changes if a
decisive assumption fails. Qualitative confidence needs a reason; arbitrary
decimal weights or a source-vote count create false precision.

## Transfer only what fits

Separate outcome criteria, platform standards, implementation choices and local
execution policy. Test a proposed rule against both the protected failure and a
nearby legitimate case. A publication ban does not imply banning local optional
files; useful history is not automatically appropriate for every audience.

Keep, adapt, reject or defer each material transfer for a concrete reason. When
the method already exists but execution or acceptance failed, fix that layer
instead of adding another paraphrase. Preserve unresolved material disagreement
and avoid reporting all open questions as defects requiring implementation.
