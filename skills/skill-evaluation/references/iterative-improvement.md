# Bounded iterative improvement

Use when an authorized method improvement needs more than one diagnostic pilot.
For a typo or an already justified narrow correction, keep ordinary authoring.
Start with one consequential decision in one skill, not optimization of the whole
collection. Reuse existing execution, inspection, analysis tools and Git. This is
not authority to create a runner, a service, a task store or self-modifying loop.

## Establish a comparable starting point

Use [evaluation design](eval-design.md) when the task distribution, grader or
measurement is new, materially changed or untrusted. Without an adequate signal,
improve the measurement or stop with a diagnostic conclusion; do not optimize
against an unreliable score.

Before the first change, keep one experiment record in the existing owner notes:

| Record | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Relevant outcome, smallest worthwhile gain, hard guards and quality floor |
| Baseline | Frozen previous bytes or no-skill condition, selected before seeing results |
| Allowed surface | Named instructions, routing placement or other explicitly authorized parameter |
| Fixed conditions | Task/measurement versions, client/model/effort, tools, permissions and context |
| Budget | Maximum candidates/trials and authorized time or usage; no unbounded retries |
| Selection | Working/selection/final roles, grouped cases and access restrictions |
| Stop | Success threshold, regression, unresolved noise, exhausted budget or defined plateau |

For cost reduction, define acceptable quality loss or required parity before the
runs; do not infer parity from a nonsignificant difference. Measure cost within
the full accepted-delivery boundary of the
[paired pilot](paired-pilot.md#inspect-four-distinct-results).

A request to design the experiment is not permission to execute it. An authorized
implementation need not invent another approval gate, but unresolved paid usage,
installation, delegation, publication or external effects retain their own limits.
When execution is unavailable, deliver the authored candidate and checks without
claiming a behavioral improvement.

## Change one explainable cause at a time

Inspect working failures, not final examples. Write a falsifiable hypothesis:
which decision is wrong, why the current method causes it, what the change should
alter and what nearby valid behavior must remain. Retain the baseline and the
last supported candidate separately. Do not silently switch either comparator.

One hypothesis may need a coherent multi-file patch, such as a routing condition
and its referenced procedure. It does not permit simultaneously changing model,
tools, grading and instructions while attributing the outcome to one of them.
When such factors are themselves authorized targets, compare them in separate
stages or explicitly report a joint intervention without a causal skill claim.

Consider subtraction and placement, not only additional text:

| Candidate | Distinguishing check |
| --- | --- |
| Remove duplicate or obsolete guidance | Protected outcome survives without its redundant process |
| Narrow a condition | Simple tasks stop acquiring work while complex cases retain the needed check |
| Move guidance to the decision point | Relevant branch actually loads and changes the action |
| Retain the current method | Evidence does not support a useful change within the budget |

Select relevant alternatives rather than requiring all of them every time.
A shorter file is not evidence of lower cost, and moving mandatory reading does
not remove it. Keep grading examples, unique incident phrases and expected answers
out of the candidate; generalize the causal rule rather than pasting failures.

For discovery changes, keep should-not-fire cases and apply the main method's
invocation-route checks unchanged.

## Inspect and decide after each candidate

Run only the predeclared, authorized comparisons in comparable fresh conditions.
Retain every attempt, failed tool call, trace and artifact; distinguish transport
or harness failures from subject defects. Vary trial/order conditions only as
planned, not until a favorable answer appears. Evaluate material guards separately
from aggregate quality or cost. Keep working and selection feedback in their
[roles](eval-design.md#separate-development-selection-and-final-evidence).

Record the patch identity, hypothesis, comparator, measurement identity, outcomes,
costs, guards and remaining uncertainty, then choose one outcome:

- **Supported within scope:** the agreed objective improves, required guards hold
  and evidence can distinguish the result from relevant noise. Keep the candidate
  for the next stage, with its actual scope rather than a universal claim.
- **Regression:** a justified guard or accepted outcome is refuted. Reject the
  candidate regardless of a better aggregate; retain evidence and the last
  supported state. Revert only task-owned changes, never unrelated user work.
- **Inconclusive:** the difference is unresolved, measurement is missing, or an
  essential condition is confounded. Do not promote it as proven or call the
  methods equivalent. Stop or propose the smallest budgeted distinguishing check.

An unchanged held-out score alone is not proof of overfitting: check the objective,
headroom and uncertainty. A cost objective can succeed at maintained quality.
Improvement on only familiar failures is a transfer warning, not a license to
keep tuning against final data.

## When progress stalls

Use the agreed plateau or budget limit, or stop earlier when plausible improvements
are smaller than the available signal. Before another patch, classify the remaining
working failures together by responsible layer, including unsatisfied task
requirements. Keep uncertain causes explicit. A recurring failure is not
automatically an instruction gap.

This diagnostic pass makes no score-improving edits. Seek a distinguishing probe
for the dominant plausible cause. An impossible or ambiguous task, or a grader
rejecting a supported answer, must be justified against the original contract
using [test-audit](../../test-audit/SKILL.md), not removed because it lowers scores.
Do not require the score to keep rising or weaken protected behavior at a plateau.

Version a legitimate measurement repair as in
[evaluation design](eval-design.md#freeze-a-usable-measurement). Unresolved
disputes stop promotion; they do not authorize an optimizer to rewrite its own
acceptance criteria.

## Freeze, validate transfer and hand back

After selection stops, freeze one candidate and compare it with the fixed baseline
on untouched final groups under the agreed budget. Do not select another candidate
or retry until green from these results; the final role's limits are in
[evaluation design](eval-design.md#separate-development-selection-and-final-evidence).

For a broader claim, check other relevant task families and client configurations
in separately identified conditions. A local gain does not establish transfer.
No extra campaign is required when the claim stays deliberately narrow.

Report the supported/regressed/inconclusive decision, exact change and measurement
versions, scope, adverse results, costs, exposure and unverified claims. Distinguish
authored, structurally checked, behaviorally exercised, comparatively supported
and published. Return those facts to the active
[research-driven change](../../research-driven-change/references/stage-handoffs.md)
when it owns delivery; evaluation neither restarts that workflow nor grants merge
or release permission. The [evaluation-design sources](eval-design.md#sources-and-transfer-limits)
explain the borrowed mechanisms and limits, not evidence that this candidate works.
