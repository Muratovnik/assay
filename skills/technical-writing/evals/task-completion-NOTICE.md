## Fictional fixtures and published exposure

The small inline cases describe fictional tools and organizations. They are source-grounded within their supplied fixtures, not observations of deployed third-party products. Existing fixture notices remain part of their exact inputs. The public cases are reusable working examples once committed; publication does not preserve an unseen evaluation partition. The original timing and exposure of a reserved case belong to this study's records, not to future uses of the public file.

## Research and exclusions

The study cites primary research and project documentation by URL. It does not redistribute the full private reference documents supplied during the task. Confirmed diagnostic web-page captures are represented by source URLs, hashes and explicit provenance records where the archive inventory identifies that transformation. Original writer documents, original project files, assessment keys, frozen methods and required finalization records are not rewritten by that operation.

Authentication data, environments and publication connector responses are outside the retained evidence scope. The archive manifest identifies every excluded inventoried file by hash, size and reason, and distinguishes an exact duplicate from a provenance-only replacement. Excluded page bodies and private documents cannot be reconstructed from those records. The study report and archive index define the evidence actually delivered and the limits of its verification.
