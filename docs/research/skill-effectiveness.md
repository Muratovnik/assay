# Assay skill comparison records

This address is retained for the link in the frozen
[catalog comparison protocol](../../skills/skill-evaluation/evals/comparison-protocol.md).
The reports describe separate studies:

| Report | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Current catalog diagnostic](skill-effectiveness-diagnostic.md) | 42 assignments with no-Assay, singleton and paired-skill conditions, plus simple controls. The canonical report records observed outcomes and unresolved evidence. |
| [PR 24's original report](https://github.com/Muratovnik/assay/blob/f951f07eb57d9d270d973a411082524d01f2b290/docs/research/skill-effectiveness.md) | 10 trials comparing no supplied Assay with the complete catalog over five workflow groups, alongside its research assessment. |

The interventions, tasks and outcome boundaries differ. Their results remain
separate and must not be pooled into a catalog effectiveness score.
